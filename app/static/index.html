1 Model storage

The speech models (about 6 GB) live on an NFS share, so runs never need the internet.

    The share is empty? Download the models

    Needs internet access from the cluster and a Hugging Face token (read). The token's account must have accepted the terms of pyannote/speaker-diarization-3.1 and pyannote/segmentation-3.0. Takes about 10 minutes.

    2 Object storage (S3)

    Recordings are read from one bucket folder and the text results are written to another.
